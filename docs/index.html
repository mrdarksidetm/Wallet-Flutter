Our Story

Built by the community,
for the community.

Project Wallet started as a simple idea: personal finance shouldn't be complicated or expensive. We wanted a tool that felt native to the phone, respected privacy, and looked beautiful enough to use every day.

Today, it's a growing open-source ecosystem maintained by developers who care about craftsmanship and user experience. No investors to answer to, just you.
